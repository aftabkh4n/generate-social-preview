# Project Examples

These references illustrate choices made for particular projects. Choose the
current project's message and composition independently.

## Open SaaS: retro editorial

![Open SaaS promotional artwork](../assets/style-references/retro-editorial.png)

Credit to [Vince Canger (@vincanger)](https://github.com/vincanger) for the
Open SaaS artwork, shared in his original post,
[How I promoted my open source repo to 6k stars in 6 months](https://dev.to/wasp/how-i-promoted-my-open-source-repo-to-6k-stars-in-6-months-3li9).

The reference illustrates strong type hierarchy, flat color blocks, and
purposeful crops. Borrow those principles without copying its branding,
wording, screenshots, palette, or exact layout.

## REA: analysis artifacts and a sequence

![REA analysis artifacts and agent workflow](../assets/style-references/evidence-workflow.png)

This reference was used for [REA](https://github.com/morluto/rea). Showing
disassembly, decompiler output, and a sequence helped communicate that
project's reverse engineering capabilities.

The reusable idea is to choose imagery that makes the project understandable.
A sequence helps when the relationship between steps matters. Other projects
may benefit more from a single illustration, interface, output, or typographic
composition. Even another analysis tool may call for a different approach.

Treat the command, code, hex values, icons, and layout as example content.
Depicted details in a new card should come from the project being featured.
